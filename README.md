# Chomp Clock (v1.1.2)

### An offline personal fasting, food calorie, and weight tracker app.

- **Fasting Tracker**: 7-stage metabolic biomarker telemetry with custom duration picker (hours & minutes).
- **UK Food Database**: Curated catalog of Tesco, Sainsbury's, ASDA, M&S, Morrisons, Aldi/Lidl, plus Cereals and Dinner Combos.
- **UK Barcode Scanner**: Instant nutrition lookups with portion scaling and meal categories.
- **Weight Telemetry**: Multi-unit tracker supporting Stone & Pounds (st & lbs), Pounds (lbs), and Kilograms (kg).
- **Data Portability**: Full .JSON and .CSV export/import and backup management.
